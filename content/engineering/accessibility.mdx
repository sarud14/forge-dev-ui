## Stance

Forge treats accessibility as a product requirement, not a polish pass. Interactive primitives wrap Radix so focus trapping, keyboard movement, and dismiss behavior are not reimplemented by hand.

## Phase 1 contract

- Dialog traps focus, restores it on close, and dismisses on Escape or overlay click.
- Select is a combobox plus listbox with typeahead via Radix.
- Tabs expose tablist, tab, and tabpanel; arrow keys, Home, and End move between tabs.
- Tooltip has `role="tooltip"` and dismisses on Escape.
- Toast is announced through a live region.
- Data Table sortable headers are real buttons with `aria-sort`.
- Input errors use `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, not color alone.

## How we check

Component tests assert roles, names, and `aria-*` attributes. Playwright plus axe-core runs on the home page as a smoke gate. Automated checks cannot prove assistive-technology quality — that claim is not made in Phase 1.
