## When to use

Use Confirmation when the action cannot be undone easily — delete, replace, discard. Do not use it for every save.

## How

Open Dialog from the triggering Button. Title states the action. Description states the consequence. Default footer is Cancel / Confirm. The caller performs the mutation on Confirm.

## Accessibility

Focus is trapped while open and restored to the trigger on close. Escape and overlay-click dismiss. The title is the dialog name.
